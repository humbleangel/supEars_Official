How a session reads

Illustration of the workflow, not a live recording. Speak, release, your words appear.

You say · Português
Me liga quando chegares em casa.
You get · English
Call me when you get home.
You say · Deutsch
Lass uns das morgen früh besprechen.
You get · English
Let us discuss that tomorrow morning.

Click the ear, or press the hotkey. Talk. Go quiet or click again — the text pastes at your cursor. Nothing to copy.

Status of the project

Plain facts. Roadmap items are marked as roadmap.

Pre-alpha v0.8.71. No public download yet.

Windows today. A single offline installer, no account, no telemetry. Your voice never leaves the machine.

On the roadmap: Mac and Linux builds, a measured accuracy figure, public downloads at alpha (0.90). No dates set.

Price: free during pre-alpha and beta testing. A paid license with a 60-day trial starts at alpha.

Questions

Short answers.

When can I download it?

At alpha (0.90). Pre-alpha builds are private. Join the waitlist and we will notify you.

Does it need internet?

Only to download the app and get your key. Transcription itself runs fully offline on your PC.

Which languages can I speak?

The 26 in the ledger above, plus auto-detect. Speak one, write another.

Mac or Linux?

Windows first. Both are on the roadmap with no date set.
