## What is new vs V4
- **Real camera rig** (`cam.js`): 23 keyframes of pan / tilt / dolly / crane across the film, with
  parallax on the backdrop (0.4 depth), deterministic handheld shake, and motion blur during fast moves.
  Signature push-through: the crest swells as a foreground object and we cut at the blur peak into the
  battle-cry card.
- **3D phrase wall** (`phrases3d.js`): 26 phrases, one per real language, flying at angles through
  three depth bands (near/mid/far sizes 64/46/34, alpha 0.95/0.7/0.42, rotY up to +-18 deg) while the
  hero line stays damped and readable — the research rule "everything moves except the thing you read".
- **Voice wall** (`wall.wav`): all 24 language clips staggered 17-34s with stereo panning, 3-6 voices
  overlapping at any moment, then settling to a quiet user-planet bed behind the use-case chapter.
- **Real use cases on screen** (`uiwin.js`): email / chat / notes windows where the English text types
  itself in right after each language line, with cursor and paste flash — speak in your language,
  it lands in English at the cursor.
- **Soundtrack mashup** (`mashup.py`): 6 of the owner's 8 phonk tracks tempo-locked to a 140 BPM grid
  (rolled-off at 0.82-1.22, two-pass verified), cut into 2-bar chunks across 35 bars, stabs at every
  section boundary, sidechain-ducked under the VO, loudnorm+limiter master. Total build: 60.000s.
