# Alt accounts under other names — what the platforms actually allow

> Researched 2026-09-15 02:50 UTC. Question from owner: can we run other accounts under other names posting animated videos/GIFs on X, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok?
> Short answer: **yes, multiple accounts are allowed — fake independence is not.** Every platform bans the same thing under different names. Internal document, not public copy.

## The hard line (all platforms agree)

Alt accounts that are **openly yours** (branded shows, language editions, format accounts with disclosure): allowed everywhere.
Alt accounts that **pretend to be independent fans/users** hyping supEars: banned everywhere + FTC liability. This is astroturfing, whatever you call it.

## Per-platform rules (primary sources)

| Platform | Multiple accounts? | The ban that catches fake-fan accounts | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | Allowed, but not for manipulation | "Platform Manipulation and Spam" + "Misleading and Deceptive Identities" — no fake identity that misleads/confuses, no artificial amplification | https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-rules |

## The law (US, applies wherever US viewers see the post)
- FTC Endorsement Guides (rev. 2023): any connection the audience wouldn't expect (employee, owner, paid, free product) must be disclosed **clearly in every post** — prior disclosures don't cover new posts. Fake likes/reviews from non-existent people: *"clearly deceptive, and both the purchaser and the seller could face enforcement."* Hiring several influencers to post identical pre-written brand posts: allowed only if truthful + disclosed.
- Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- Note: UK/EU have parallel rules (ASA/CAP, DSA) — same disclosure principle.

## What we CAN do (safe playbook, $0)
1. **Branded show accounts** — e.g. "26 Tongues — a supEars show", bio links supEars, disclosure in bio + first posts. A format account with a different name is fine when affiliation is stated.
2. **Language editions** — supEars PT-BR, supEars Hindi etc. Standard localization practice, zero deception.
3. **Founder account** — personal voice, bio says builder of supEars, every product post discloses.
4. **Repost/UGC accounts** — repost real user clips *with permission and credit*; never invent users.
5. **Employee/advocate posts** — anyone on the team posts only with "I work on supEars" disclosed.
6. **Contests/duets** — real users, real disclosures, one specific ask per video.

## What we must NEVER do
- Fake "independent user" personas praising supEars (all 5 platforms + FTC).
- Same pre-written hype posts across supposedly unrelated accounts.
- Comment/like pods or mass "check out this app" comments (YouTube explicitly names this).
- Buying likes/followers (FTC: buyer AND seller liable).
- One personal account per human on Facebook — no second "persona" profile, use a Page.

## Bottom line
Different names: yes. Different *identities pretending independence*: no — that single trick risks channel bans on YouTube, CIB takedowns on Meta, suspension on X/TikTok, and FTC exposure, all for content we could post legally from disclosed accounts anyway.
