TL;DR — the market in one paragraph

Every direct rival is cloud + subscription + account. Wispr Flow and Willow charge $15/mo ($144/yr); SuperWhisper, Aqua and Voicy cluster at ~$8–9/mo with lifetime options near $250; Dragon charges $699 once and is dying on desktop. All free built-ins (Win+H, Apple, Docs, Gboard) are cloud-tethered, app-locked, and quit on you (timeouts, raw transcripts, no translate). supEars is the only offline Windows float with translate-to-paste, no account, and a 60-day trial (4× the 14-day norm). Our wedges: offline privacy, Indian/ESL accents + Hinglish, subscription fatigue, and Windows-first simplicity.

4 · Pricing — anchors, norms, our posture

The anchors (2026, primary sources)
"$15/mo for dictation" = Wispr/Willow Pro — everything is framed vs that. $8–9/mo (SuperWhisper, Aqua, Voicy, Otter-annual) feels "cheap". $24–30+ feels "justify it". Lifetime anchor ≈ $250 (≈3-yr breakeven). ≤$150 lifetime (Voice In, MacWhisper €64) feels "indie impulse". Trial norm: 14 days — our 60-day plan is 4× the norm, a genuine standout (word-cap it). No-credit-card-upfront is the norm.
How we sell it (no payment infra yet)
Start on Lemon Squeezy (5% + $0.50 all-in, VAT/GST handled, license keys, $0/mo). Avoid Gumroad (~13–21% effective) and Stripe-alone (you file VAT yourself). Posture: ~$7–9/mo or ~$70–85/yr + ~$199–249 lifetime (undercut the $250 anchor); 60-day word-capped trial, no CC; small recurring free allowance (not Aqua's 1k-lifetime dead end) to build habit.

5 · Their wounds — top 6 weaknesses with our counter

Sourced from Reddit, G2, Trustpilot, forums, HN (16 complaint threads). Internal only — counters never name rivals.

  1. "My voice leaves my PC" — privacy fear

    Cloud transcription, screenshot/telemetry storage, meeting bots recording strangers (Otter class-action Aug 2025). Counter: "Offline .exe. Your voice never leaves this PC. No account, no cloud."

  2. Subscription fatigue + tiny free caps

    2,000 words/wk ≈ 13 min of speech ("burned by Tuesday"); $99–180/yr; Trustpilot 2.7. Counter: "Free while in testing. Talk all day. No meter anxiety."

  3. Built-ins quit on you

    Win+H mic dies <60s, Apple's 30-sec auto-stop, cloud "connection issues", post-update breakage. Counter: "The ear waits while you think. Hold, speak, paste — Windows-first."

  4. Indian/ESL accents + Hinglish mangled

    +30–50% WER on code-switch, wrong-script output, "must enunciate, can't converse". Counter: "26 talk languages with explicit Hinglish mode. Built for real Indian English."

  5. Mic fragility + silent regressions

    Bluetooth cuts first words, "mic works everywhere but here", one update dropped accuracy 9→11% WER, auto-cleanup rewriting words unasked (700+ hate-responses). Counter: "Pick a mic once. Predictable paste. No surprise rewrites."

  6. Abandoned or expert-only

    Mac/Home/mobile lines EOL'd, $699 + weeks of voice training, 8 weeks to learn scripting. Counter: "Windows now. 2-minute install. No training, no code. Just dictate."
