supEars — per-language transcription precision needs

Prepared 2026-09-15 04:09 UTC · From dev research (docs/whisper_models.html, journal #69–#71) for the marketing agent

Honesty rule — read first. Every figure below comes from third-party published benchmarks on public test sets (Common Voice, Fleurs, MLS, VIVOS). They are not supEars measurements. Our measured claim stays exactly what it is: targeting 97% measured accuracy, with per-language measurement at alpha. Never present these numbers as our results; present them as why each language gets its own precision plan.

How to read this

Base WER = word error rate of the stock multilingual base model on that language (lower is better; CER for Chinese/Japanese where noted). Need = how much that language gains from its dedicated upgrade pick. Every language below has a drop-in upgrade path — no engine change needed for any of them.

What this means for messaging

  • Every language has a path to excellence — the story is never "language X is bad", it is "language X gets its own precision pick". Biggest wins are the Tier 3 comebacks (Bengali 55.5→9.65, Hindi 102→~5–13, Swahili ~39 vs ~100 base).
  • Small-model story: 244M fine-tunes match or beat 6×-larger stock in sv/id/ur/vi/th/hi/ar/sw — precision without giant downloads. Users never see model names (Settings shows Fast/Balance/Precise only).
  • Do not promise: per-language accuracy numbers as ours, dates, prices, downloads. Those come at alpha with real measurements.

* WER = word error rate on public sets (Common Voice / Fleurs / MLS / VIVOS as published by model authors). CER where character-based scripts make WER meaningless. “—” = no published stock figure; tier by upgrade-path strength.
