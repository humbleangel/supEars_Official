TL;DR — 8 organic moves that matter most

  1. Launch the 26 Tongues clip series START NOW

    Same sentence dictated in a new language daily — tease/waitlist CTA, zero download needed. The single highest-leverage format: it demos the product, the languages, and the offline claim in 20 seconds.

  2. Answer, don't advertise: Super User + Quora + r/RSI START NOW

    Real answers with disclosure and demo GIFs. These threads rank on Google for years — evergreen visibility no ad can buy.

  3. Own the writer pipeline: NaNo/sprint demos + KBoards later START NOW

    "Dictate your 1,667 words in 15 minutes" — timed word-sprint videos where volume-writers already gather.

  4. Post everywhere natively, 4×/week from 1 shoot START NOW

    Clean 9:16 master, native upload per app, rewritten captions. Never cross-post with a TikTok/Shorts watermark — suppression is documented.

  5. Open the Facebook Group + YouTube + X fronts START NOW

    Group seeding + Shorts search tail + X reply-game. Three owned audiences compounding to alpha.

  6. Speak to the 1B+ non-native English writers START NOW

    "Speak your language, paste perfect English" — the biggest audience on earth for this product, and nobody owns it.

  7. Recruit VibeCoders with Prompt Loud START NOW

    Dictating prompts into Cursor/Claude is at-cursor dictation already. They are loud, online, and amplify.

  8. At alpha: unlock downloads, press, and creator keys AT-ALPHA

    Press tips, 26-language creator demos, forum showcase threads, Product Hunt — all gated on the live .exe. List is warm by then.
