# Is the floating ear unique? (owner question)

> Filed 2026-09-15 10:46 UTC. Owner's claim to verify: "nobody has this floating ear thing — similar apps exist (e.g. 'Orb'), this is one of our differences." Internal document, not public copy.

## Verdict in one line

**Half right.** A *floating always-on-top dictation control* is now a standard pattern — pills, bars, islands, orbs, bubbles. A **floating EAR** appears nowhere. So the differentiator is not "floating", it is the **ear as the form and the metaphor**.

## Finding 1 — The floating overlay itself is not unique (the owner's "Orb" instinct is right)

Nearly every serious desktop dictation app now floats something over your work. Shapes found:

- **Floating orb** — VoicePad (Android; calls its overlay "Float Orb"), Voxa (desktop "floating orb", local-first, open source), plus many hobby GitHub projects. "Orb" is the generic AI-voice shape of this era.
- **Floating pill** — OpenWhisper ("a small floating pill that lives on your desktop", Win/Mac-ish).
- **Floating island** — Talky ("floating island UI that stays out of your way", Windows).
- **Floating bar / widget** — Wispr Flow, Willow, Aqua, SuperWhisper (menu-bar level) — the category norm.
- **Floating dictation orb on Android** — a 2026-08-28 dev.to write-up literally titles it "How I built a floating dictation orb on Android"; Orpheus (GitHub) floats a draggable glass orb beside the keyboard; VoicePad's "Orb" is the same idea.

Conclusion: **the floating layer is table stakes.** Any of these could claim "floating". It does not differentiate us.

## Finding 2 — The floating EAR is unclaimed (the owner's real point is correct)

Searches for an ear-shaped or ear-branded floating dictation control returned **nothing**. Every overlay is an orb, a pill, a bubble, an island, a bar, or a generic microphone dot. Nobody uses the ear, and nobody uses "ear" as the name.

So the defensible difference is three layers deep:
1. **Form** — an ear, not a generic blob. Recognisable in a screenshot, in an app list, in a Short.
2. **Name** — supEars is the only "ear" in the category.
3. **Metaphor** — pairs with the name layers already filed (`2026-09-15-1040-UTC-brand-name-insights.md`): *sup?* (hello) · *super ears* (compliment) · *appears* (the function) · *disappears* (offline = you vanish from the industry). An ear means *listening* and *hearing the user's own language*; an orb means nothing.

Conclusion: **unique enough to be our visual signature.** "The floating ear" can be a phrase we use about ourselves, because no one else can.
