## Lessons for us

1. **Distribution is the product's second half.** Our channels + feedback-video loop is the moat, not a chore. Never let the repo sit silent.
2. **No single point of voice.** One blocked X account = total silence for him. Our multi-rail (YouTube, TikTok, IG, FB, TG, WA, Pinterest) exists exactly for this. Never depend on one account.
3. **Parked handles are invisible.** His Instagram handle exists and does nothing — same as our parked state. Accounts only count once they post.
4. **Platform gap favors us.** macOS-only, Apple-Silicon-first: India's masses run cheap Windows laptops, not Macs. Our Windows-first + 15-year-old-notebook CPU story serves the people he cannot reach. Complementary, not colliding.
5. **Take his benchmark discipline, not his silence.** Measured WER on a frozen set, honest retirements — same spirit as our "no % until measured" rule. Good craft; copy the honesty, not the quiet.
6. **Single product focus.** He splits across ZenVoice + ZenPense (finance app). We do one thing. Note it, keep it.
7. **For dev (suggest, never order):** the upstream Hindi fine-tune (Oriserve Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish-Prime, Apache-2.0, ~550h noisy Indian audio, 32 WER vs 62 base on Common Voice hi) exists and is license-clean. Q7 language builds could evaluate it. Dev decides.
