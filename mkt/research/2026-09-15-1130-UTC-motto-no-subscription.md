# Motto idea: "escape the trap of the subscription" (owner insight)

> Filed 2026-09-15 11:30 UTC. Owner observation: most competitors are subscription-based, so a motto for us could be built around escaping subscriptions. Internal ideation + one blocker. No public copy.

## Does it make sense? Yes — with one condition

The *insight* is sound. From our competitor map (`2026-09-15-0425-UTC-competitors.html`), the category is dominated by recurring billing:

- Wispr Flow ~$15/mo · Willow ~$15/mo · Aqua ~$10/mo · Voicy ~$8.49/mo · SuperWhisper ~$8.49/mo.
- Subscription is the default; only some offer a lifetime escape hatch (SuperWhisper ~$250, Voicy ~$260).
- Industry trial norm is **14 days**; we offer **60**.
- MacWhisper proves the demand: users pay **once (€64)** for offline privacy instead of renting it. That was one of the five lessons we took from them.

So "no subscription" is a real, evidenced wedge — not a slogan invented in a vacuum.

**The condition:** the motto is only true if our paid license is a **one-time / perpetual purchase**. Product truth currently says only "a paid license with a 60-day trial starts at alpha." It does **not** yet say whether that license is one-time or recurring. **Blocker: owner must confirm the license model before this motto appears anywhere public.** A "no subscription" promise we cannot keep is the one mistake worth avoiding.

## Wording

"escape the trap of the subscription" carries the right idea but reads awkwardly in English and leans on the word *trap* (confrontational; the handoff forbids attacking rivals, and a fight-picking motto undercuts the calm of the rest of our voice). Cleaner options, ranked:

1. **No subscription. Ever.** — shortest, absolute, memorable. Best if the model is truly one-time.
2. **Buy once. Own it.** — states the benefit, not the enemy.
3. **Escape the subscription trap.** — the owner's line tightened; keeps the "escape" metaphor.
4. **Pay once. Keep your words.** — ties the model to the privacy story.

Recommendation: lead with **#1 or #2**, keep **#3** as a supporting line. Attacks on rivals stay out.

## How it fits the rest of the brand

- Pairs with the "disappear" layer from the name insights: offline = you vanish from the industry; no subscription = you never feed the billing machine. Same idea twice: **we are the absence.**
- Fits the story project: the solo dev against the giants — the giants rent, we sell once.
- Honest, checkable, and it is a feature difference rather than a claim about our accuracy or completeness.

## Rules noted

- No public use until the license model is confirmed (blocker above).
- Never name or attack competitors; the motto speaks about *us*, not them.
- Do not combine with price figures or dates — marketing never announces a price.

## FINAL — owner decision 2026-09-15 11:37 UTC, finalized 11:39 UTC

**Motto is: "Escape the Subscriptions Traps"** (owner's wording, verbatim — keep the plural exactly as written, capital S on Subscriptions, capital T on Traps, no `~`). My "No auto-renewal. Ever." recommendation is rejected; do not re-argue it. Keep "no auto-renewal, ever" only as a *supporting proof line* under the motto, and "rent vs own" for the forever tiers.

Recorded not as a debate but as fact: the paid tiers are prepaid per period ($4.99/1mo, $15/6mo) with no auto-renewal, so the motto's claim is about the **trap** (auto-renewing subscriptions), which we do not have. Owner accepts the wording risk; the decision is his.
