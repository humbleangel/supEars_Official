# Launch schedule: 10/10 countdown (owner idea, corrected)

> Filed 2026-09-15 19:05 UTC. Owner proposal: launch **October 10** (10/10 — "10 out of 10"), with a daily regressive-countdown video series up to launch. Videos to be "user comments about dependence on the alpha," written by us if real comments are unavailable. Internal plan. No public post.

## Verdict

- **The date and the countdown: keep.** 10/10 is memorable, free to use, and a daily countdown is the cheapest way to build a habit and an audience before alpha. Strong instinct.
- **Fabricated user comments: cannot do.** Not a style objection — a hard stop. Reasons:
  1. **FTC Endorsement Guides** treat invented testimonials as deceptive advertising (our own `2026-09-15-0250-UTC-alt-accounts-policy.md` already bans fake fans for exactly this reason).
  2. **Platform rules** — YouTube/X/Meta/TikTok all ban deceptive content and coordinated inauthentic behaviour; a fabricated-comment series is the textbook case.
  3. **Timing makes it false on its face:** alpha 0.90 is the *first* public test round. Before it, there are no users, so there is nothing to quote.
  4. It would also poison the one asset this product actually has: credibility about privacy. A brand whose pitch is "no server, nothing to retain" cannot open with invented people.
- Note the difference between **fiction we own** and **fake people we invent.** The past-tense dev-journey series is fine — it is narrative, framed as story. A quote attributed to "a user" is an impersonation, and that is the line.

## Improved mechanic (legitimate, same energy)

Three replacements for the fake-comment videos. All fit a countdown; mix them.

1. **The 24-day dev log (recommended for the countdown body).** One short per day, dated, first-person, from the build. Already the tone of the story project. Ends on launch day. Honest, cheap, and it *is* the countdown.
2. **Promise cards (recommended for the "why should I care" beat).** Our own statements, presented as ours, never attributed to a person:
   - "No data leaves your computer." — that's the line the owner liked; make it supEars speaking, not a user.
   - "Escape the Subscriptions Traps." (motto, §3)
   - "No account. No telemetry. No server."
   - "The floating ear."
   - "$4.99 a month, or $50 forever." (locked prices, cleared)
3. **Real testers, once alpha opens.** If testers send feedback, ask permission in writing, quote with a first name/initial they approve, and say it is a tester. Real + consented beats invented, and it fits the tail of the countdown (launch day + after).

Do **not** use "illustrative" or "sample" quotes that look real; that is the same trap with a disclaimer, and it still reads as fake.
