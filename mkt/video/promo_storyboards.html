Approved voice, use verbatim: "The Ear that understands your language." · "Speak in your own native language." · "Escape the Subscriptions Traps" · "Stop feeding the MegaCorps." · "no auto-renewal, ever" · "rent vs own". Never: competitor names, accuracy %, download promises before alpha, dated Mac/Linux claims.
