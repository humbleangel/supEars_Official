Questions

Asked, answered.

Short answers to common questions.

Which languages can I speak?

26 — English, Portuguese (BR + PT), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Czech, Hungarian, Greek, Romanian, Swedish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Urdu, Arabic, plus auto-detect. Speak one, write another.

How does the free trial work?

Free during pre-alpha/beta testing, everything unlocked. Testers get 60 days. No account, no credit card.

How do I get 60 days free?

Join the test team and tell us what you think. Your 60-day key arrives by email.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the app and get your key. Transcription itself runs fully offline on your PC.

Where does my text go?

Wherever your cursor already is. Nothing to copy or move.

What if it mishears me?

Fix it by hand, or re-paste from history. Nothing is ever lost.

Will it record my music?

No. System sound mutes while you speak, restores after.

What happens when the trial ends?

Recording pauses until you add a license. History and settings stay.
